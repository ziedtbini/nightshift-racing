Support

Help for Nightshift Apex Racing on iPhone and iPad.

The game does not start

Restart the app, confirm that iOS is up to date, and close memory-intensive apps before trying again.

Sound or haptics are missing

Check the in-game sound control, your device volume and iOS haptic settings.

Notifications

Notification access can be changed at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Nightshift Apex Racing.

Contact

Open a support issue in the Nightshift Apex Racing GitHub repository. Do not include personal, payment or account information.
