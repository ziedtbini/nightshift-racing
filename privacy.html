Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Nightshift Apex Racing uses limited technical data to operate, improve and monetize the game.

Data processed

  • Gameplay and app interaction events through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash diagnostics, device and app-version information through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Push-notification tokens through Firebase Cloud Messaging when notifications are enabled.
  • Advertising identifiers, consent choices and ad-performance data through Google AdMob, subject to device settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • Scores and preferences stored locally on the device.

Purposes

Data is used to understand game performance, diagnose crashes, deliver requested notifications, prevent fraud and display advertising.

Choices

You can disable notifications in iOS Settings. Where required, an advertising consent form is shown before ads are requested. Tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Service providers

Firebase and AdMob are services provided by Google. Their processing is governed by Google's applicable privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local game data can be removed by deleting the app. Diagnostic, analytics and advertising data follow the retention controls of the respective service providers.

Children

Nightshift Apex Racing is not directed to children under the minimum digital-consent age applicable in their country.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support channel linked on the Nightshift Apex Racing website.
